# SuperKman64.github.io
Have YOU ever wanted to play NES games for ANY device? Well now you can! With my new nes emulator powered by emulatorJS, you can play NES games anywhere! On your laptop, on any school device, on your phone*, anything! You have to put in an nes rom you legally own on your computer. But there are a lot of rom hacks online.


*Note: EmulatorJS always crashes your tab in safari so if you want to play this i suggest using chrome as your best option.
